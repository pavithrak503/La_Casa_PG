# Privacy Policy for La Casa PG

**Effective Date: May 20, 2025**

La Casa Groups ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **La Casa PG** mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Data Collection and Use
While using our app, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information:
*   **Personal Information:** Name, Phone Number, and Room Details.
*   **KYC Information:** Identity proof documents (e.g., Aadhar, ID) for resident verification and security.
*   **Financial Information:** Payment records and transaction history related to PG dues and deposits.
*   **Usage Data:** Information on how the app is accessed and used.

## 2. Purpose of Data
We use the collected data for the following purposes:
*   To provide and maintain PG management services.
*   To process and track rent, deposit, and utility payments.
*   To manage and resolve tenant complaints.
*   To facilitate communication regarding announcements, polls, and food menu updates.
*   To ensure the security and safety of all residents through KYC verification.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored securely using **Supabase** (PostgreSQL) with industry-standard encryption. We take reasonable steps to protect your data; however, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use Supabase as our backend service provider. They maintain their own privacy policies regarding data handling and storage. We do not sell or trade your personal data to outside parties.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
